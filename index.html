About the garage

A Main Street workshop that stands behind its work

Central Garage is Otautau's own auto shop — the kind of place where you can drop the car off, have a yarn about what's wrong, and trust it'll be done properly. We look after locals, farmers and everyone passing through the Aparima district.

No upsell, no jargon. We tell you what's actually wrong, what it'll take to fix, and get you back on the road.
