What we do

Your rural, all-round workshop

Standard road vehicles, farm machinery and lifestyle block equipment — serviced and repaired in Otautau, Western Southland.

01

Standard automotive & light commercial

  • Warrant of Fitness inspections

    WOF inspections and compliance repairs for vehicles, trailers and road motorbikes.

  • Utes & 4x4s

    Routine servicing, suspension repairs and upgrades, and heavy-duty clutch replacements suited to rugged farm terrain.

  • Brakes & tyres

    Supply, fitting and balancing of off-road tyres, plus heavy-duty braking components.

  • General diagnostics

    Scan-tool diagnostics to troubleshoot engine management issues.

02

Agricultural & implement repairs

  • UTV, ATV & motorbike servicing

    Service and repairs for all makes and models of motorbikes. We carry a selection of filters, oils and parts.

  • UTV, ATV & implement tyres

    Motorbike and farm trailer tyres suited to Southland conditions, puncture repairs and a selection of tubes.

  • Specialised welding

    Welding for farm gates, trailers and milk sock cages, plus plastic welding for calf feeders and similar equipment.

03

Small engines, farm & lifestyle equipment

  • Grounds care

    Service and repairs for push mowers and ride-on mowers.

  • Pumps & generators

    Service and repairs for water pumps, generators and a variety of small motors.

Not sure what you need? Give us a call and describe it — we'll tell you what's involved before any work starts.

About the garage

In the Froude family since 1973

Keith and Thelma Froude have owned and run Central Garage (Otautau) Ltd since 2005. The business has been in the Froude family since 1973.

We are an established rural mechanical garage in Otautau, Western Southland. Our versatile, all-round workshop services a wide range of standard road vehicles and local farm and lifestyle equipment.

  • Family owned & operatedKeith and Thelma Froude, owners since 2005.
  • MTA membersCentral Garage (Otautau) Ltd is a member of the Motor Trade Association.
  • A versatile rural workshopCars, utes, 4x4s, motorbikes, trailers and small engines.
