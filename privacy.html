Privacy Policy

How Central Garage Otautau handles the personal information you share with us.

This is placeholder wording provided for the website demo. Central Garage Otautau should have its final privacy policy reviewed before going live.

1. Introduction

Central Garage Otautau ("we", "us") respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand). This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we look after it.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you call or enquire — such as your name, phone number and vehicle details.
  • Information about the work you ask us to carry out on your vehicle.
  • Basic, non-identifying website usage data if analytics are enabled on this site.

This website does not include a contact form — enquiries are made by phone — so we do not collect information submitted through online forms.

3. How we use it

We use your information to quote and carry out work, contact you about your vehicle, keep service records, and meet our legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We only share information where necessary — for example with parts suppliers or sub-contractors involved in your job, or where required by law. We ask anyone we share it with to protect it appropriately.

5. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and to hold it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

6. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Contact us using the details below.

7. Third-party services

This site embeds Google Maps and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may collect limited technical data under their own privacy policies. Following a link to Google to leave a review is subject to Google's privacy policy.

8. Contact

To ask about your information, call us on 03 225 8537 or visit 145–147 Main Street, Otautau 9610.
